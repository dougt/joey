Kangaroo - Wikipedia · en.wikipedia.org · 2007-10-23 14:02
A kangaroo is a marsupial from the family Macropodidae (macropods, meaning "large foot"). In common use the term is used to describe the largest species from this family, the Red Kangaroo, the Antilopine Kangaroo, and the Eastern and Western Grey Kangaroo. Kangaroos are endemic to the continent of Australia.
